1. Home

Welcome to Student Text Hub

Student Text Hub is an imagined marketplace for students where students can easily and affordably purchase textbooks for sale, rent, and borrow.

The concept of Student Text Hub attempts to solve the challenges that students face when it comes to accessing textbooks because of expensive prices. Rather than purchasing all required textbooks from bookshops, our platform allows students owning such books to provide them to others through selling, borrowing, or renting.

2. About Us

History of the Organisation

Student Text Hub is a fictitious organisation which was established on the realisation that the use of textbooks may lead to high expenses for students. The core philosophy of this organisation is that a market should be created through which students may exchange textbooks among themselves.

Rather than letting textbooks remain unused after the semester, the books may be provided by one student to another who needs the book. The organisation will initially focus on universities and colleges, and then expand from there.

Mission Statement

"To facilitate the availability and affordability of academic books through a system where students can interact for buying, selling, lending and renting textbooks."

The mission statement focuses on three main aspects: affordability, accessibility, and cooperation amongst students. It is imperative that the website does not pose any difficulty for students in accessing required textbooks.

Vision Statement

"To be the premier student textbook exchange portal that offers affordable, accessible and reusable academic books."

The vision statement defines the end goal for the organization: creating a sustainable market for textbooks which can be used by multiple students.

3. Services

We provide four primary methods for students to manage their course reading materials:

  • Buying Textbooks

    Purchase pre-owned textbooks directly from fellow students at affordable rates.

  • Selling Textbooks

    Post advertisements for books you no longer need after the semester to recover some of your expenses.

  • Renting Textbooks

    Rent textbooks for a specific semester or subject period at lower temporary costs.

  • Borrowing & Lending

    Connect with peers to lend or borrow academic materials directly within your campus network.
